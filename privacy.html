Privacy policy

Last updated 6 November 2026

ShotDrop is a desktop app that runs entirely on your own computer. We (OZ Tech Innovations, the developer of ShotDrop) don't operate any servers for it and don't collect, receive or store any of your data. There is no account, no analytics and no tracking.

What stays on your computer

  • Your photos, their ratings, flags and labels, and the library database.
  • Your settings, including camera FTP logins.
  • Sign-in tokens for cloud storage you connect, such as Google Drive or Dropbox. These are kept in a configuration file in ShotDrop's data folder on your computer.

Google Drive

If you connect Google Drive, ShotDrop asks Google for the drive.file permission by default. That lets ShotDrop:

  • create folders in your Drive and upload your photos into them;
  • list and read only the files and folders that ShotDrop itself created, so you can choose a destination folder and sync can tell what's already uploaded;
  • read your account's email address and storage quota, shown in ShotDrop so you know which account is connected and how much space is left.

ShotDrop can't see or change any other files in your Drive. If you choose the optional "All my folders" mode instead, ShotDrop asks for full Drive access so you can pick an existing folder. It only uses that access to list folders and upload your photos.

Your Google data goes directly between your computer and Google. It's never sent to us or anyone else, never used for advertising, and never used to train AI or machine-learning models. Nobody at OZ Tech Innovations can read it.

ShotDrop's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Other connections ShotDrop makes

  • Storage you choose: Dropbox, a NAS, an rclone remote or another drive, only when you add them as destinations.
  • Remote viewing, if you turn it on: ShotDrop can start a Cloudflare tunnel so you can view the gallery from your phone. That traffic goes through Cloudflare, protected by a PIN you set.
  • One-time downloads: helper tools and a face-detection model, fetched from their public download locations such as GitHub and Google's model storage.

Removing your data

Disconnect a cloud account in ShotDrop to delete its sign-in token from your computer. You can also revoke ShotDrop's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Deleting ShotDrop and its data folder removes everything it stored. Photos already uploaded to your storage stay there until you delete them.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the date above. For questions, open an issue at github.com/terraseraph/ShotDrop/issues.
