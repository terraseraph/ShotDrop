ShotDrop portable data folder.

Because this folder sits next to ShotDrop.exe, ShotDrop keeps everything here instead of in
%APPDATA% and Pictures: settings and the database (app\), preview cache (app\cache) and the photo
library (ShotDrop\). Move or copy the whole ShotDrop folder to take it to another PC.

Delete or rename this folder to make ShotDrop use %APPDATA%\ShotDrop and Pictures\ShotDrop instead.
