Compare: Inspect dialog + Before/after slider

Mockup only (nothing built yet). Placeholder gradients stand in for photos. Same tokens and .tb / .key / .seg as the app. Screens 2 and 3 are lightly interactive: drag the divider, toggle the buttons.

1 · Entry points on the Compare screen

Each pane gets an expand button (top right, next to ✕), and double-clicking a pane opens it too. I or Space inspects the highlighted pane. When exactly two frames are compared, a Before / after button appears in the toolbar (also B). With 3+ frames, selecting two and pressing B works too; it uses the highlighted pane and the next one.

2 · Inspect dialog

Near-fullscreen sheet over a scrim. The image fills the canvas (Fit by default). Zoom: Z toggles Fit / 100%, mouse wheel / pinch zooms toward the cursor, click toggles zoom at that point, drag pans, 0 back to Fit. ← → step through the compared frames (the filmstrip below shows them; click one to jump). The right panel has histogram, clipping, sharpness and EXIF. Linked zoom option keeps zoom/pan when stepping so you can flick between frames at the same spot. Esc closes. The Compare with… button starts the slider with a second frame.

3 · Before / after slider

Two frames, pixel-aligned, same zoom and pan. Drag anywhere to move the divider (or the round handle). ← → nudge 1%, ⇧← ⇧→ nudge 10%, Home / End jump to an edge, 0 recentre. S swaps sides, V flips horizontal/vertical. Zoom and pan work as in the inspect dialog, applied to both images at once. Labels show file name plus key EXIF; differing values are highlighted amber. Starts from the toolbar when two frames are compared, or from the inspect dialog via Compare with… (small picker below, showing the compared frames first).
