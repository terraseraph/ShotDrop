Shoot on the camera. See it on screen seconds later.

ShotDrop is a free Windows app that turns your PC into a wireless landing zone for your camera. It shows every frame full-screen as it arrives, lets you cull while you shoot, and copies your photos to your own storage in the background.

Download for Windows

What it does

  • Camera to screen: a built-in FTP server receives each shot over Wi-Fi from most modern mirrorless cameras.
  • Cull as you go: rate, flag and label frames while the shoot is still happening.
  • Sync to your storage: copy photos to Google Drive, Dropbox, a NAS or another drive. Nothing passes through our servers; we don't run any.
  • Runs on your machine: no account, no hosted service, nothing to pay for.

Google Drive

If you choose Google Drive as a destination, ShotDrop asks Google for permission to create files in your Drive and manage only the files and folders ShotDrop itself creates. It uploads your photos there and can list those folders so you can pick where photos go. It can't see the rest of your Drive. Details are in the privacy policy.
